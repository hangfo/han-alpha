# Roadmap

## V0.1 - Directly runnable core

- Synthetic data.
- Three strategies.
- Deterministic agents.
- Risk engine.
- Simulated broker.
- IBKR adapter skeleton with bracket orders.
- Ledger, API, CLI, backtest, tests.

## V0.2 - Real research data

- Polygon or Databento provider.
- Corporate actions and delisted universe.
- SEC submissions and XBRL ingestion.
- FRED/ALFRED vintage ingestion.
- Point-in-time symbol master.
- Dataset snapshots and hashes.

## V0.3 - Research validation

- Walk-forward runner.
- Purged/embargoed validation.
- Cost and market-impact scenarios.
- Parameter perturbation.
- Benchmark dashboard.
- Champion-Challenger registry.

## V0.4 - IBKR paper operations

- Full broker reconciliation.
- Open-order recovery.
- Execution and commission reports.
- Scheduled nightly reset recovery.
- Shadow fill comparison.
- Telegram or authenticated mobile approval.

## V0.5 - Domain alpha

- AI supply-chain event ontology.
- Earnings revision extraction.
- Industry bottleneck propagation.
- Analyst-estimate revision data.
- Catalyst verification over one to four quarters.

## V1.0 - Controlled live pilot

- Separate host or hardened service.
- Authenticated control plane.
- Hardware-backed secret storage.
- Small live capital and lower risk limits.
- Human approval.
- Automatic rollback and operational SLOs.
